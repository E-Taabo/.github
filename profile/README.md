# E-Taabo ⚽

**Book a pitch. Find a match. Play.**

E-Taabo is a football platform built in Hargeisa, Somaliland. We help teams and players book fields, organize matches, and pay in a few taps, so more time goes to playing and less to phone calls.

## What we're building

- **Field booking:** pick a field and a time, and pay from your in-app wallet.
- **Teams:** create a team, build your roster, and manage your lineup.
- **Matchmaking:** find opponents and fill open spots in matches.
- **Tools for field owners:** a simple dashboard to manage fields, prices, and bookings.

## Status

E-Taabo is in active development.
